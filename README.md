# BiomedicalInstrumentation_CARDIOARK_Project
The immediate postoperative period following cardiac surgery with cardiopulmonary bypass is marked by a significant incidence of cardiac arrhythmias, affecting 35-50\% of patients. Early detection and management of these arrhythmias are crucial for improving patient outcomes and safety. Despite their prevalence, there is limited understanding of the incidence, risk factors, and treatment of early postoperative arrhythmias. In response to this critical need, we present CARDIOARK, a wearable device designed for real-time monitoring and diagnosis of arrhythmias in postoperative cardiac surgery patients. This prototype addresses the need for continuous monitoring, ensuring early detection and management of arrhythmias, thereby enhancing patient outcomes and safety during the vulnerable postoperative period.
